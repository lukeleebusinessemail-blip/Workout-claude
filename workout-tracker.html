Eating 2,000 leaves a 735 kcal deficit (~1.5 lb/week); the extra cardio and steps below carry the rest of the way to 1.7. Protein at 185 g is well above the 148-156 g on your scan printout: at this rate of loss, protein is the single thing standing between you and losing muscle along with the fat, and 185 g is ~1.3 g per pound of your 143.5 lb lean mass. Keep training heavy in the 5-8 range on days 1-3 — on a deficit, load preserves muscle, not extra volume. Weigh in daily, judge only the 7-day average. Six-day split
