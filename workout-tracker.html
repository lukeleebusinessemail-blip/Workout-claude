Your scan measured TEE at 2,735 kcal — lower than the 2,890 I estimated from height and weight, so the real numbers replace the guess. Eating 2,000 leaves a 735 kcal deficit (~1.5 lb/week); the extra cardio and steps below carry the rest of the way to 1.7. Protein at 185 g is well above the 148-156 g on your scan printout: at this rate of loss, protein is the single thing standing between you and losing muscle along with the fat, and 185 g is ~1.3 g per pound of your 143.5 lb lean mass. Keep training heavy in the 5-8 range on days 1-3 — on a deficit, load preserves muscle, not extra volume. Weigh in daily, judge only the 7-day average. Six-day split

Push / Pull / Legs run twice: days 1-3 are the heavy pass (5-8 reps, longer rests), days 4-6 the volume pass (10-15 reps, short rests). Rename anything, drag the numbers, add or delete lifts and whole days — the tracker follows whatever is here.
